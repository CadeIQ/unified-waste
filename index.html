FAQ

Straight answers.

Anything else, ask us when you request your assessment.

Is the market assessment really free?

Yes. Send us one recent invoice and we’ll compare each service with the market, flag fees and contract terms worth knowing about, and tell you whether you’re caught by any new rules. No cost and no obligation. If you’re already on a good deal, we’ll say so.

How is Unified different from other brokers?

Most brokers are paid to sign you up and then step back. We manage your waste every day after that: we check every invoice line, chase every issue, take your services back to market every quarter and show you all of it in your portal.

How do you check every invoice line?

Every supplier invoice is checked automatically against your agreement and your collection records: rates, lift counts, bin sizes, levies, fees and duplicates. Anything that doesn’t match is reviewed by our team and disputed with the supplier. You can see every check in your portal.

How do you get paid?

Our fee is built into one all-in price for each service. There are no separate management, account or admin fees.

I’m still in a contract. Can you help?

Yes. We find your end date, notice period and rollover terms, then time the switch so you don’t pay twice or miss the notice window.

Do you work with multi-site and national businesses?

Yes. Every site sits under one account, one portal and one monthly invoice, across every state and territory.
